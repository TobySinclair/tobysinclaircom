## What AI business coaching is

AI business coaching is coaching pointed at the people side of AI adoption. The client is usually a senior manager, an HR or talent leader, or an exec team. The work is the conversations AI forces: role anxiety, resistance, new expectations, restructuring, and the quiet non-use that never shows up on a licence dashboard.

It is not "how to write better prompts", though prompting shows up. It is not a product demo. It is the same coaching craft I have practised for a decade, aimed at the hardest change most leaders will face this decade.

## Why AI adoption is a people problem

Every company is "doing AI". Few are ready for what it does to identity, skill, and trust.

The licences get bought. The town hall happens. Then managers are asked to lead a change they have never led, in a language they don't yet speak, with teams who can tell when the leader is bluffing. That gap is not technical. It is human.

This is why AI coaching for leaders is not a nice-to-have next to the IT rollout. The rollout fails in 1:1s, in skip-levels, and in the meetings where nobody says they are scared of looking stupid.

## What coaching for AI change looks like

One-to-one coaching for the person carrying the change. Team coaching for the exec group that has to tell a story people will believe. And rehearsal of the specific conversations: a role that is shrinking, a high-performer who will not touch the tools, a policy that sounds fine on a slide and cruel in a meeting.

The method is ordinary coaching: listen, ask, don't rush the advice. The territory is new. I am not a consultant who read about AI. I ship with large language models every day, and I know where they help and where they quietly fail.
