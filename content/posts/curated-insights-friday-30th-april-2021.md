Successful people don't need coaching. 🏆

What do you think?

Coaching can be mistakenly associated with "fixing" people and that only "underperformers" need coaching.

In the book, [Trillion Dollar Coach](https://www.tobysinclair.com/post/book-summary-trillion-dollar-coach-by-eric-schmidt-silicon-valley-coaching), Eric Schmidt former Google CEO shares the power of coaching. It's a fascinating inside look at how the worlds top leaders use coaching.

Eric Schmidt is famous for saying "everyone needs a coach" and how it's been crucial in his ongoing success.

## **Atomic Coaching: Improve Self-Awareness 🎯**

Each week I share a challenge to help you improve your coaching skills: active listening, asking questions and self-awareness.

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/67d4e8_e36357bafdad431bb2f067432b60968d~mv2.png)

### **This Weeks Challenge 🤹**

> "There are three things extremely hard: steel, a diamond, and to know one's self." Benjamin Franklin, 1750

Self-Awareness is one of the core skills of coaching. It helps you listen, be more present and be better in tune with the person or group you are coaching.

[This HBR Article](https://hbr.org/2018/01/what-self-awareness-really-is-and-how-to-cultivate-it) explores what self-awareness is and how it can help you be a better leader.

In particular, the article defines two types of self-awareness:

- *internal self-awareness*, represents how clearly we see our own values, passions, aspirations, fit with our environment, reactions (including thoughts, feelings, behaviours, strengths, and weaknesses), and impact on others.
- *external self-awareness* means understanding how other people view us, in terms of those same factors listed above.
