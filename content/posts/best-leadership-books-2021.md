## **Best Leadership Books 2021**

In 2021 I read 50 Books. The first thing you might be wondering is "How does he read so many books?" [I shared my technique in this article.](https://www.tobysinclair.com/post/how-to-read-more-books)

Many of the books I read in 2021 were on the topic of Leadership. I'm always trying to learn to become a better leader. Every year new books are released. Some good, some bad.

I've created a shortlist of the 10 best leadership books I read in 2021.

Highlights from these best leadership books include:

**Conflict:**

> Relationship conflict is generally bad for performance, but some task conflict can be beneficial: it’s been linked to higher creativity and smarter choices.

[Adam Grant - Think Again](https://amzn.to/3shDvvb)

**Relationships**:

> Most relationships are not exceptional. It is exceptional relationships that enable people to succeed in life and work. The challenge - exceptional relationships are exceptionally hard to develop.

[Carole Robin and David Bradford - Connect](https://amzn.to/3miJeNI)

**Listening:**

> To really listen is to be moved physically, chemically, emotionally, and intellectually by another person’s narrative.

[Kate Murphy - You're Not Listening](https://amzn.to/33I7Okz)

**Wealth:**

> People who live far below their means enjoy a freedom that people busy upgrading their lifestyles can’t fathom.

[The Almanack of Naval Ravikant by Eric Jorgeson](https://amzn.to/3J1ocgk)

**Product Teams:**

> Give teams problems to solve, rather than features to build. Empower them to solve those problems in the best way they see fit.

[Marty Cagan - Empowered](https://amzn.to/3F9H3mZ)
