## **💡 3 Big Ideas From Corporate Escapology by Adam Forbes**

**1. Redefining Work: Escape the Corporate Cage Without Quitting**

Many professionals feel trapped in their jobs, but escaping doesn’t always mean leaving. [Adam Forbes](https://amzn.to/3QymEzk) argues that the real escape is about mindset—creating autonomy within your current role by focusing on value creation over compliance. Instead of waiting for permission, leaders should carve out meaningful work, build influence, and operate with an entrepreneurial spirit. This means optimising your role to play to your strengths, setting boundaries against low-value tasks, and actively shaping your career rather than drifting through it.

**2. Think Like an Entrepreneur (Even Inside a Company)**

Traditional corporate structures encourage compliance and incremental progress, but real career growth comes from thinking like an entrepreneur. [Adam Forbes](https://amzn.to/3QymEzk) encourages leaders to approach their work as if they were running a business within a business. This means identifying unmet needs, taking ownership of outcomes, and creating leverage through strategic relationships. By adopting an entrepreneurial mindset—experimenting, taking calculated risks, and delivering value beyond your job description—you gain greater control over your work and career trajectory.

**3. Strategic Influence: The Key to Freedom in the Workplace**

[Corporate escapology](https://amzn.to/3QymEzk) isn’t just about avoiding bureaucracy but mastering influence. Leaders who can shape narratives, build alliances, and navigate office politics with integrity gain the freedom to work on what truly matters. Forbes highlights that real career success isn’t about working harder but working smarter—understanding power dynamics, knowing when to push back, and ensuring your efforts are recognised. You can create a role that gives you fulfilment and autonomy by strategically positioning yourself as indispensable and aligning your work with the organisation’s priorities.
