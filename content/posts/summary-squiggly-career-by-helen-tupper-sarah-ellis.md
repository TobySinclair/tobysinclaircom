## **💡 3 Big Ideas From Squiggly Career by Helen Tupper, Sarah Ellis**

1. **Leverage Your Unique Talents**: Helen and Sarah emphasise the importance of identifying and utilising your "super strengths" – the unique talents that you excel in and enjoy. In a squiggly career, these strengths are more valuable than ever. By focusing on what you’re naturally good at and what energises you, you can navigate career changes with more confidence and create a path that is both fulfilling and successful.
2. **Build Strong Networks**: The book underscores the importance of building a diverse and supportive network, not just within your industry but across different fields and disciplines. Effective networking involves both giving and receiving value, whether through mentorship, advice, or shared opportunities. A robust network not only opens doors but also provides diverse perspectives and support, which is crucial for thriving in a non-linear career path.
3. **Embrace Sideways Moves and Reflection**: "The Squiggly Career" advocates for the power of lateral moves – shifts that may not seem like traditional promotions but offer new skills, experiences, and opportunities. Alongside this, regular self-reflection helps you stay aligned with your values and strengths, making conscious career choices that lead to sustained growth and satisfaction.
