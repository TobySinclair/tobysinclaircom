[Atomic Habits at Work](https://www.tobysinclair.com/post/how-to-use-atomic-habits-at-work-the-big-ideas-for-organisations?&utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=curated_insights_atomic_habits_problem_solving_new_experiment&utm_term=2021-01-07)

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/67d4e8_3b13ffd2fd23411da6e9d54321f8808c~mv2.png)

This summary is curated for leaders, coaches and consultants within organisations.

Big Ideas include:

1. Tiny improvements lead to remarkable results
2. Focus less on goals, OKR's and outcomes. Focus more on systems.
3. Change who you are by what you do.
4. Design the Environment to make change obvious.
5. Use social dynamics to make change attractive.
6. Enable consistency to make change easy.
7. Reward desired behaviours instantly to make change satisfying.

### **2. A quote:**

> "I cut knots instead of laboriously trying to untie them"

[Keith Johnstone | Impro: Improvisation and the Theatre](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/306940.Impro?&utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=curated_insights_atomic_habits_problem_solving_new_experiment&utm_term=2021-01-07)

My insight: Many organisations spend time trying to untie knots (adjusting existing processes) rather than cutting knots (taking radical action)

**Do you untie or cut knots in your organisation?**
