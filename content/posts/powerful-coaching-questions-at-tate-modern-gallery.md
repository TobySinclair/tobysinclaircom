Asking Powerful Questions is one of the foundations of Coaching.  Asking questions that go beyond what is obvious enables the Coachee to think more deeply about a Goal or Challenge.

The Tate Modern [Start Display](http://www.tate.org.uk/visit/tate-modern/display/start-display) has some great Powerful Questions inviting the visitor to go beyond the obvious and think more deeply about the art on display. This certainly provoked my own deeper thoughts.
