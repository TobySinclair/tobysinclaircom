⭐ Toby's Rating: 8/10

## Should you read this?

> "Everything is about to change. Soon you will live surrounded by AIs... None of us are prepared."

I read this to understand AI. I finished it more worried about biology.

If you lead people through technology change and still think AI is the whole story, read it. It will prepare you. It might also scare you.

## 3 Big Ideas from The Coming Wave

**Big Idea 1 - Biotech is the part most leaders are still not looking at.** We've all heard about AI. Suleyman's point that landed for me is synthetic biology: a single person today likely "has the capacity to kill a billion people." The wave is two technologies, not one. Leaders who only brief on ChatGPT are watching the wrong horizon.

**Big Idea 2 - Containment is the job.** These technologies spread faster than anyone can govern them. For a workplace leader that is not an abstract policy debate. It is the tension between using AI for advantage and putting real limits around it — jobs, privacy, what you will and will not automate — before the tool makes the decision for you.

**Big Idea 3 - Access is not the same as control.** As the tools get cheaper, more people can build, copy, and deploy them. That flattens power inside and outside the organisation. The response is not a training day. It is deciding who gets the tools, what they are allowed to do with them, and what happens when they go wrong.

## Best quotes from The Coming Wave

> “Over time, then, the implications of these technologies will push humanity to navigate a path between the poles of catastrophe and dystopia. This is the essential dilemma of our age.”

> “Within the next couple of years, whatever your job, you will be able to consult an on-demand expert… It is a leap in cognitive potential at least as great as the introduction of the internet.”

## Toby's takeaway

The coming wave is AI and synthetic biology.
