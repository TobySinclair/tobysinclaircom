##### Whats included?

Here is what is included within the program

8 x Live Training Sessions (32hrs total)

Each live training session will blend a mix of theory and practice. In a dynamic learning environment, you'll learn quickly the science and practice of coaching. All training is delivered via Zoom.

10hrs Coach Mentoring

After completion of the live training session, every student will receive mentoring. This enables you to get real-time feedback, explore tricky scenarios and get advice on your options. 6hrs will be completed in a group setting and 4hrs one-on-one.

## 10

32hrs Guided Learning

I've distilled the best ideas from 100's leadership and coaching models into small, actionable challenges. The program consists of 30 coaching challenges. These are designed to be small in size but big on impact. You'll complete these in your own personal notion workspace.
