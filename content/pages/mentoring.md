What is Coach Mentoring?

​

The International Coaching Federation (ICF) defines Mentor Coaching as providing professional assistance in achieving and demonstrating the levels of coaching competency. Furthermore, Mentor Coaching means an applicant (mentee) being coached on their coaching skills rather than coaching on practise building, life balance, or other topics unrelated to the development of an applicant’s coaching skill.

​

How can Coach Mentoring Help?

​

Coach Mentoring can help you with the following:

​

1. What is the real difference between Agile Coaching and Professional Coaching?

2. How to become a Professional Coach?

3. What is the best Coaching Program for me?

4. How to build up coaching hours?

5. What are some cheaper, free alternatives to completing a coach training program?

​

How does Coach Mentoring work?​

​

My Coach Mentoring is conducted via Email. I've found that often common questions and queries can be answered without the need to talk in person.

I like to think of myself as a compass. Through my years of experience, I can usually direct you to helpful information quickly without you needing to trawl through the confusing messages across the internet.

All you need to do is drop me a question below and I'll answer usually within 24hrs.
